# Repository instructions

- Pocket Requiem owns the stage generator, the models, the effects and the three.js reference (`web/`), the simulation core (`crates/requiem-sim`), the compiler and the pack format (`crates/requiem-cook`, `crates/requiem-pack`), the Vita runtime (`vita/`), and the PSP and 3DS runtimes (`crates/requiem-handheld`, `psp/`, `n3ds/`). PocketJS (pinned in `vendor/pocketjs`) owns the device toolchains, the Vita dev host and wired debug transport (`tools/vita-dev.ts`, `tools/vita-usb.ts`), VPK packaging and the GXM device kernel (`devices/vita/pocket-vita-gxm`).
- Do not edit the submodule to fix application behavior. Send reusable changes to PocketJS, then update the pinned revision here.
- **This repository stays private** until its owner says otherwise. Do not publish the repository, a build, a pack, captures or models to a public place.
- Use Conventional Commits for commits and pull requests (`type(scope): summary`). Publish validated changes as a Draft PR.
- One rule set, one implementation: gameplay and every figure's motion live in `crates/requiem-sim` and nowhere else. The reference runs it as wasm (`bun tools/requiem.ts sim`), the compiler links it to sample the knights' clips, the console links it natively. Do not re-implement a rule or a pose in TypeScript or C.
- `requiem-sim` and `requiem-pack` build without `std` (`default-features = false`): `core`, `alloc` and `libm` only.
- The snapshot layout and the shared constants are generated: after changing `abi.rs`, the bone list in `skel.rs`, the clip table in `knight.rs`, the move table or the effect kinds, run `bun tools/requiem.ts sim` (it rewrites the ignored `web/src/sim/abi.gen.ts`).
- A pose is a `Key` (`anim.rs`): feet, trunk, and the place and direction of the prop; the solver places the limbs. Author a swing as the path of the weapon. Look at a new clip with `bun tools/requiem.ts shot --query "model=…&poses=…"` before it goes to a device.
- The knights' clips are stored frames on every device. A change to a clip's length or frame count is a change to `knight::CLIPS`, and the pack must be compiled again; the simulation and the pack must come from the same revision.
- The stage derives from one seed. No `Math.random`, wall time or canvas APIs in `web/src/world`, `web/src/model` or `web/src/fx`: the same code runs in the browser and in Bun for the export.
- Every static surface samples one atlas (`web/src/world/atlas.ts`), so a cell is one draw. A new surface kind is a new strip in that atlas, not a new texture or program.
- An effect is layers of FxIR (`web/src/fx/ir.ts`): each lowers to a vertex template with every component in [-1, 1] and eight rows of constants. A new look is a new layer in `effects.ts`. A new shape is a new program, and it is written three times with the same formulas: `web/src/render/fx.ts`, `vita/shaders/fx_*.cg` and `crates/requiem-handheld/src/fx.rs`.
- A change to the stage, a model, an effect, the atlas or the bake goes through `bun tools/requiem.ts cook`; the pack and its compile receipt are outputs under the ignored `.pocket-build/`. A device difference is a profile value or a lowering in `crates/requiem-cook`, not a branch in a generator.
- The Pocket3D title card plays first at every launch, on each console and in the reference, before the renderer starts: `pocket3d_title::vita::play()` in `vita/src/main.rs`, `title()` in `psp/src/main.rs`, `pocket3d_title_play()` in `n3ds/src/main.c`, `playTitle()` in `web/src/main.ts`. It comes from PocketJS (`vendor/pocketjs/engine/pocket3d/crates/pocket3d-title`): do not skip, shorten, recolour or redraw it here, and do not draw the mark with the game's own renderer. A development run on the Vita skips it with `{"title": false}` in `boot.json`; a `?shot` capture and a `?model` preview of the reference do not show it.
- Keep captures, logs, cooked packs, USB-share contents and build receipts in `.pocket-build/`. Put reproducible commands, results and limits in the PR description instead of committing them.
- Separate evidence kinds when reporting: host build, compile receipt, reference capture (`shot`), device frame timing (`bench`), and what a person saw or felt on the console. A capture is not a judgment of hand feel.
- Shader programs are compiled on the device by SceShaccCg and cached by source hash under `ux0:data/pocket-requiem/gxp`. Mixing `half` and `float` in `lerp`/`smoothstep` is an overload ambiguity there: cast explicitly. A program that does not read an attribute has none to bind: `program_blends` skips it.
- The frame budget is 33.3 ms on the Vita at 960 × 544. The vertex stage is the limit: the full army program places about 19 000 triangles a millisecond and the far-rank program 40 000. A renderer change reports `bench` before and after; a change to a vertex program that the army or the field runs needs that measurement first.
- The Vita reads the pack whole into the heap before uploading it (`_newlib_heap_size_user` in `vita/src/main.rs`); a pack that outgrows the heap stops the load. If the process stops answering, the console must be brought back to Pocket Devkit by hand before a new binary can be pushed.
- The PSP and the 3DS share `crates/requiem-handheld`: what a frame draws is decided there, and a device crate only draws it. A rule both machines need goes in that crate with a host test; `cargo run --release -p requiem-handheld --example probe -- <pack>` reports what a frame holds without a device.
- The handheld packs hold no ground mesh. The ground is the simulation's height grid and the `GRND` colour grid; `ground.rs` builds patches into slots. A change to the ground's look is a change to the generator's ground layers and to the bake, never a mesh in the pack.
- The PSP's limit is the GE: about 31 000 triangles a frame at 30 frames a second with this mix (skinned mage, morphed knights, textured ground). The 3DS takes 80 000. A change to a handheld profile's distances or to the knights' budget reports `bun tools/psp.ts bench` or `bun tools/n3ds.ts bench` before and after.
- On the PSP, write a frame's CPU-computed vertices through `Scratch` (`psp/src/gfx.rs`) and flush them before the draw: `sceGuGetMemory` returns display-list memory, which is addressed past the data cache. `psp/Cargo.toml` builds every crate at opt-level 3; built for size, a system call with five arguments passes the fifth one wrong.
- PSPLINK: `bun tools/psp.ts run` resets PSPLINK and waits for `usbhostfs_pc` to log the new connection. That needs the `usbhostfs_pc` started by `bun tools/psp.ts serve`; one started elsewhere has no log, and a command sent before the cable is back leaves PSPLINK without storage until it is restarted on the console.
- 3DS installs are `.3dsx` over the paired wire (`bun tools/n3ds.ts install`); never a CIA. The pairing key is under the submodule's ignored `.pocket/3ds/devices`. A PICA instruction reads one input register: copy a second attribute to a temporary first. A source's swizzle is applied before the write mask picks components.
- A device has a microsecond clock for the simulation's parts (`Sim::clock`, `Crowd::clock`, read back as `simMs` and `armyMs` in the PSP's status). Measure before changing the army's tick: its cost on the PSP was a cache footprint and a per-tick rate, neither visible on a computer.
